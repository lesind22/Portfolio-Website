To be active over the summer, I decided to stay active in the field, aside from my daily coding schedule. So, I applied for the 2025 Wood Family Fellowship at Drayton Hall Plantation, located in Charleston, SC. It is a fellowship to strengthen my museum studies skills and offer the institution the digital techniques I've learned throughout the semester. I know that there are a lot of artifacts that not only are not digitized but need a place within their collections, and that is what I am most excited about—creating a story for the voiceless and giving them a resting place that recognizes the historical significance of Drayton Hall Plantation. The Fellowship announcement will be on May 15th, 2025. Regardless of the results, I know it will not deter me from my career goal. Good luck to all applicants

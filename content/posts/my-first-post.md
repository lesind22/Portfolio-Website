Welcome to my personal website! 

Hello, welcome to my academic website! I am Indira Lessington, a PhD student at Clemson University studying Digital History. My interests are 19-20th century African American History with a concentration on film, media, and photography. While an undergrad, I knew that the focus of African American Studies had to be centered not only on something I could relate to but also on understanding, interpreting, and conveying to students. I hold a BA in African/African American Studies and an MA in History with a concentration in Museum Studies.

  

I am from Charleston, SC, a history-rich city now riddled with gentrification and out-of-town contractors. Like other historians, it is my duty to keep the culture alive and take care of the written history. I was born and raised around the Gullah/Geechee diaspora, and I love to hear stories of childhood and other stories of resilience, strength, and faith.

  

Since I was a child, I've been fascinated with African American culture, which led to my digital dissertation project that focuses on JET and Ebony magazines, their effect on African American representation, and the demise of social respectability/respectability politics. Aspiring to be a digital cultural historian, I believe in moving to the next level of keeping students engaged, software digital techniques must be accessible and digestible for the next generation. If you are interested or have experience, please reach out! I'd love to chat!
